Anniversary guide from A to Z

Tap a keyword to open the details.

Getting there

The party takes place at Mica’s Garten, Badenerstrasse 790, 8048 Zurich. There is no parking at the venue and very little public parking nearby, so please come by public transport, bike or shuttle bus.

  • Tram or bus to the “Zürich, Micafil” stop – directly opposite the entrance.
  • Bike racks are available at the entrance.
  • The shuttle bus from Wohlen stops a 6-minute walk from the venue.
Points of contact

If you have questions, speak to the reception crew or anyone wearing a “Staff” lanyard.

Attractions
  • Photo booth (1st floor): Grab a few people and capture your Jubi moment.
  • Karaoke: Sing, sing, sing…
  • Face painting (until 20:00): For anyone who fancies a glow-up.
  • Silent party (1st floor): Live electronic music from DJ “Knightbeat”, party hits or a voting channel – you decide what you dance to on your headphones.
  • Games zone for young and old (until 20:00): Jump around, do crafts or take part in the anniversary games.
Data protection / photos

Photos and videos will be taken for internal and external communication. By taking part, you agree to this.

Entrance / access

There are access checks at the entrance. Only registered guests may enter – this also applies to children you bring along. Because capacity was planned carefully, we unfortunately can’t accommodate walk-in visitors.

Check in with your first and last name; your partner and children register under your name as well. You’ll then get your wristband.

First aid

Injured or feeling unwell? Get help at the bar under the tower (next to the big staircase).

Family depot / breastfeeding & nappy-changing tent

On the neighbouring site there is a supervised depot for prams, pushchairs, child seats and nappy bags. Right next to it is a separate area for nappy changing and breastfeeding.

With the “Baby Pass” you can quickly get to the tent and the depot through the rear exit and back again. Ask the security staff at the rear exit for it and return the pass afterwards.

Food

Savoury or sweet, with meat or vegan, for adults or kids – there’s something for almost everyone. And: it’s available while stocks last.

Tip: it gets particularly busy between 18:00 and 20:00 – if you can, eat before or after. For allergies or special requests, please talk to the food stall operator directly.

Lost and found

Found or lost something? Ask at reception. Anything not collected by the end of the party is taken back to the office.

Cloakroom

There is no cloakroom. You can leave bulkier items at the supervised depot (see family depot).

Hearing protection

We don’t provide hearing protection – bring your own earplugs if you want them. Children’s ears are especially sensitive, so we recommend hearing protection for kids.

Anniversary games

Compete against other guests in the games zone. The top performers each receive two lounge chairs, straight from the anniversary venue. Winners are announced after the party via the anniversary calendar.

Protection of minors

We strictly follow the rules on protecting minors when serving alcohol. The wristband helps the bar staff keep track of guests’ ages.

Prams & pushchairs

The grounds are gravel paths and space is limited: it’s best to leave your pram or pushchair at home, or park it at the family depot.

Opening hours

The venue is open from 15:00 to 23:30. Food, drinks and party run until 23:00.

Smoking

Smoking is not permitted in the games zone, near the toilets or on the neighbouring site.

Treating others with respect

Please be considerate – there are babies and children on site. And as always, wherever we are: we treat each other with respect. More information.

Animals

Animals are not permitted on the festival site.

Weather

The party goes ahead even if it rains; part of the site is covered by tents. If it rains we recommend a rain cape; if it’s sunny, remember sun cream and a hat.
